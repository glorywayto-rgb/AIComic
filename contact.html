ABOUT US

I am a story writer and creator who brings original ideas, characters, emotions, and worlds to life through storytelling. Every story begins with my own imagination and is written by me. I create the characters, develop their personalities, build their worlds, shape their emotions, and write the dialogues that bring each scene to life. I personally edit and arrange the stories, add the dialogues and text bubbles, and create the final presentation. For the visual side of my stories, I use AI as a tool to help generate images. The storytelling, creativity, editing, and final vision remain my own. My goal is simple: **to create stories that don't just tell you what happens, but make you feel it.** I hope that every story takes you somewhere new, introduces you to unforgettable characters, and leaves you with an emotion, a thought, or a memory long after you've finished reading.

I sincerely apologise for using AI in the creation of these stories. AI is a tool I use to help bring my ideas and imagination to life, but the stories, concepts and creative direction come from my own imagination.
